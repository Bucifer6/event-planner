# Romanian regional invitation designs

The Rustic and Traditional ranges each include one design from four regional craft references. The artwork is drawn for EventDesk. The palettes and compositions are contemporary interpretations.

| Region | Craft reference | Rustic | Traditional |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maramureș | Carved timber gateways and their solar and star motifs. [National Heritage Institute museum catalogue](https://ghidulmuzeelor.cimec.ro/idEN.asp?k=430) | Carved Gate uses an arch and worked posts. | Solar Rosette sets ornaments into a formal border. |
| Bucovina | Wax-resist egg decoration with lines and lozenges. [National Museum of the Romanian Peasant](https://www.mntr.ro/en/diverse-obiceiuri-) | Waxwork Bands uses pale linework and a row of diamonds on a dark ground. | Lozenges uses red and cream with corner geometry. |
| Oltenia | The pottery and spiral decoration of Horezu. [UNESCO](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/craftsmanship-of-horezu-ceramics-00610) · [Romanian National Commission for UNESCO](https://www.cnr-unesco.ro/uploads/media/f782__2023-patrimoniu-imaterial-engl.pdf) | Clay Spirals uses warm clay colours and a free-flowing rim. | Glazed Medallion uses concentric plate-like rings and corner scrolls. |
| Transylvania | Painted floral panels in Transylvanian Saxon furniture. [Emil Sigerus Museum collection study](https://biblioteca-digitala.ro/reviste/CIBINIUM/10-CIBINIUM-muzeul-astra-2009-2010_194.pdf) | Painted Chest uses a floral side panel and asymmetric layout. | Floral Panels uses a formal painted frame and a central composition. |

## Clothing and woven textiles

These designs redraw the geometry and colour relationships of documented examples. Colour values are approximations for the invitation palette. Museum photography and contemporary artists’ artwork are not embedded in the cards.

| Design | Documented reference and design treatment |
| --- | --- |

## Romanian embroidery and weaving collection

The invitation borders are drawn as repeating stitch grids. The charts and textiles below inform the shapes, colour order and arrangement. The invitation compositions and digital stitch grids are adaptations of those references.

| Invitation | Reference |
| --- | --- |
| Muscel Checked Collar and Altiță Crosses | The collar band and shoulder panel on plate 1 of Elena C. Cornescu’s 1906 album use repeating red crosses, black checks and gold details. [Plate 1 reproduced by Semne Cusute](https://semne-cusute.blogspot.com/2012/10/ie-din-muntenia-muscel.html) |
| Comănești Red and Black Diamonds and Black Sprigs | The red-and-black diamond repeat and dark branching motifs on E. Kolbenheyer’s Comănești plate 22. [Comănești plate](https://dragusanul.ro/wp-content/uploads/Broderie-22-Com%C4%83ne%C8%99ti.jpg) |
| Rășinari Woven Stripes | The layered red, dark blue, cream and muted green bands of the ASTRA ștergar 845 T. [Rășinari catalogue record](https://clasate.cimec.ro/Redirect.asp?k=b2a17931b29343668fa934998cde67f6) |
| Oltenia Facing Birds | The paired birds and red-violet field documented in the Dolj carpet inventory, together with the facing birds around a cup on the Slătineanu scoarță. [Dolj inventory](https://clasate.cimec.ro/omc/OMC-2026-2010-01-26.PDF) · [Slătineanu carpet](https://clasate.cimec.ro/Redirect.asp?k=4BBBB32F19964EA68DADF4A18F2A2588) |

### Ten additional variations

The collection also includes two further Muscel layouts, three Comănești layouts, two Rășinari stripe compositions, a Moldavian stepped wave, northern Moldavian loom diamonds, and an Oltenian flower-and-vine border. The new compositions use the references above for the embroidered motifs and woven registers. The placement of the bands around the invitation copy is independent of their position on the original garment or textile.
